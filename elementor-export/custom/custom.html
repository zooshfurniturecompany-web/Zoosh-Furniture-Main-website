Methodology

The Custom Crafting Process

Every custom order travels through a structured design and building cycle to guarantee precision.

01

Design Consultation

Collaborate with our designers to sketch concepts, define proportions, and align with your interior blueprint layouts.

02

3D Rendering & CADs

Our studio converts concepts into three-dimensional rendering models, allowing you to review geometries and details before fabrication.

03

Material Selection

Select from premium solid wood timbers—including aged Kerala Teak Wood, Mahogany, and imported Ash Wood—complemented by natural rattan cane weaves and premium upholstery fabrics.

04

Master Construction

Our veteran carpenters craft the piece manually, leveraging traditional joineries (mortise & tenon) for structural supremacy.

05

White-Glove Delivery

The custom furniture piece is packed inside wooden crates and delivered directly to your room with full installation assistance.
